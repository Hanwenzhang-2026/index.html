Abstract

This study, titled Traces of Time and Culture: Revitalizing the Silk Road’s Legacy through Modern Art, explores mixed-media art as a contemporary approach to preserving and revitalizing Silk Road cultural memory. Through acrylics, plaster, sandy mediums, and mineral pigments, the research examines how artistic materials represent time, erosion, and transformation found in historical artifacts. Employing interdisciplinary methods combining art theory, material culture, and heritage studies, the study integrates fieldwork, material experimentation, and artistic documentation.
